Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Literary Lab may collect through this website, how it is used, and how to contact us about privacy-related questions.

Information We Collect

We may collect contact details you submit through forms, email links, or direct inquiries, including your name, email address, WhatsApp number, manuscript details, project requirements, and any files or notes you voluntarily send.

How We Use Information

We use submitted information to respond to inquiries, assess project scope, prepare quotes or readiness reviews, coordinate services, and improve the clarity and performance of the website and client communication process.

Cookies and Basic Analytics

The site may use basic cookies, embedded services, or analytics scripts if they are added for performance, verification, or traffic reporting. These tools may collect technical information such as browser type, pages viewed, and referral source.

How Long Data Is Kept

Inquiry records may be retained for business follow-up, project history, legal compliance, or operational reference. We do not claim a fixed universal retention period for every message or project file.

Third-Party Services

Forms, email delivery, GitHub Pages hosting, and messaging or analytics tools may process data outside this website. Those platforms have their own privacy practices, and use of those services is subject to their respective terms.

Your Requests

If you want to ask about your submitted information, request an update, or request deletion of inquiry data where reasonably possible, contact us at hello@literarylabstudio.com.

Contact

For privacy questions related to this website, contact Literary Lab at hello@literarylabstudio.com or visit literarylabstudio.com.
