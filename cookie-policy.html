Cookie Policy

This page explains how cookies or similar browser technologies may be used on the Literary Lab website for technical operation, measurement, and embedded services.

What Cookies Are

Cookies are small browser-level data files used to remember preferences, support analytics, or help third-party services understand page activity and performance.

How They May Be Used

Cookies may be used for site functionality, analytics, script verification, embedded widgets, or performance monitoring when those services are installed on the site.

Third-Party Tools

If analytics, marketing scripts, form tools, or social integrations are added, those providers may set their own cookies according to their own policies and consent requirements.

Your Browser Controls

You can usually control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some features or embedded tools may behave differently if cookies are blocked entirely.

Related Policies
